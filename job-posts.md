## What I learned

After reading these 10 job posts, I noticed that entry-level design engineer roles are hard to find.
Only 2 out of the 10 ask for around one year of experience. Six are for senior, staff, or principal roles, while the other two ask for 3+ years of experience.
My takeaway is that companies may prefer people who can work with little guidance, especially on smaller teams where there may not be much time to train someone new.
That’s just my interpretation, though. The job posts don’t directly say this.

Location is the second barrier. Six of the 10 limit where you can work, and Nigeria is not on any of those lists. Only four look open to me: Dart, Anima, Joy and RevenueCat.

The skills that repeat most are React, TypeScript, coded prototypes and AI-assisted development. I can prove those with my API, my authentication project and my portfolio. I cannot prove performance, accessibility, user research or code review yet, so I will build those into Rota and Matinee. My strategy is to focus my messages on the open, junior-friendly roles like Anima and show shipped work instead of years of experience.
